## 1. 奖励函数定义

$$R = \sum_{i=1}^{8} w_i \cdot r_i$$

## 2. 各分量推导

### 2.1 配送距离奖励

$$r_1 = -\alpha \cdot d_{current}$$

### 2.2 配送时间奖励

$$r_2 = -\beta \cdot t$$

### 2.3 碰撞惩罚

$$r_3 = \begin{cases} -\gamma & \text{if collision} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

### 2.4 能耗惩罚

$$r_4 = -\delta \cdot E$$

### 2.5 目标到达奖励

$$r_5 = \begin{cases} +\epsilon & \text{if reached} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

### 2.6 协同奖励

$$r_6 = \eta \cdot C_{coop}$$

### 2.7 安全距离奖励

$$r_7 = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } d > d_{safe} \\ -\theta & \text{if } d \leq d_{safe} \end{cases}$$

### 2.8 边界惩罚

$$r_8 = \begin{cases} -\kappa & \text{if out of bounds} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
